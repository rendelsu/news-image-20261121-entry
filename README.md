One newspaper page that sums up Saturday 21 November 2026: the world court
finds Myanmar guilty of genocide, Russian missiles hit Poland, Wall Street
falls into correction, and the first Trump–Xi–Putin summit ends thin — 50
stories in all, with a UTC timeline, a world map with 10 numbered pins, and a
DATE CHECK footer.

- `entry.png` — the newspaper, 1024×1024, rendered locally with Python/PIL.
- `main.ts` — standard-library-only Node program; it copies `entry.png` to
  `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte.
- `layout.json` — the actual counts rendered: 50 stories, 4 heroes, 12 cards,
  10 map pins, 10 timeline marks, 30 tail items, DATE CHECK, 16 sources.
